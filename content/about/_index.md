## Introduction

Hugo Agent Zero (HAZ) is a human-friendly, forward-thinking Hugo **theme-work** for developers, agencies, and projects that want to use AI and still keep control.

We care about joy for clients, developers, and agents — a lighter alternative to feature-bloat and tech for its own sake.

**Client-friendly. Developer-focused. Agents love it.**

When we choose what to build, we use **The Client Test**: is this as client-friendly as possible?
