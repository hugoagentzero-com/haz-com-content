{{< haz_do add="callout" type="go" title="Mission Statement" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
To craft a human-friendly but forward-thinking Hugo theme-work for developers, agencies, and projects that want to leverage AI and deliver consistent results, without conceding full control to agents.
{{< /haz_do >}}

{{< haz_do add="callout" type="important" title="Purpose Statement" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
To champion a static-site publishing tool that brings joy to clients, developers, and agents. A tool that is an alternative to the feature-bloat and "tech for tech’s sake" obsession of too many similar solutions.
{{< /haz_do >}}

{{< haz_do add="callout" type="focus" title="Vision Statement" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
When a project's needs are within HAZ's sweet spot, HAZ wants to be considered a viable, lighter-weight and lower-cost alternative to WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, Framer, and the like. HAZ simply wants to be in the conversation when it's possibly the right tool for the need.
{{< /haz_do >}}

{{< haz_do add="callout" type="tip" title="Guiding Light" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
**The Client Test** guides product decisions. When in doubt ask, "Is this as client-friendly as possible?" While we want the technology to let developers and agencies feel as little restriction as possible, we also understand it's the clients who decide the value of the solution.
{{< /haz_do >}}

{{< haz_do add="callout" type="love" title="Values Statement" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
1. **Mobile-first** — The small screen experience comes first, as does a dedication to page speed and other such metrics.

2. **KISS (Keep It Stupid Simple)** — From zero required dev tooling to leaning on established standards (e.g., Simple.css), HAZ chooses proven approaches and productivity over the latest shiny new objects.

3. **DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself)** — Emphasize reusable components and configuration over new construction. When a project spawns new components, those can be folded back into the core product's library, and will then be available to all other projects using HAZ core.

4. **Accessibility** — Make it part of the process. Always try to be mindful of the full range of needs of the wide spectrum of users on the internet.

5. **Accept appropriate compromises** instead of chasing the gods of optimization, only to get a lightly rounder wheel.

6. **Robust features, calm defaults** — Offer a robust range of features and settings but mitigate decision making with defaults that cover the most typical use cases. Allow defaults to be easily overridden.

7. **Let Hugo be Hugo** when Hugo needs to be Hugo. HAZ is a mind-expanding supplement.
{{< /haz_do >}}

{{< haz_do add="callout" type="peace" title="Mantra" class="haz_force_site_primary" >}}
Better to do a great job for the few than a merely good job for the many.
{{< /haz_do >}}
